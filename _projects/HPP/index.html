HPP: Hierarchical Programmatic Probing for Long Video Understanding by Decoupling Perception and Reasoning

Awais Rauf1   Ahmed Hassan2   Greg Slabaugh3
1Queen's University Belfast  ·  2AMD  ·  3Queen Mary University of London
Paper Code Trajectories BibTeX

TL;DR: Vision-language models perform fine-grained perception and multi-step temporal reasoning jointly in latent space, limiting their ability to plan and execute multi-step strategies over long videos. We show that decoupling the two and reformulating long video understanding as iterative programmatic probing of a hierarchically segmented video yields substantial improvements. With GPT-5-mini and Qwen3-VL-2B, HPP reaches 66.4% accuracy on LongVideoBench while using up to 16× fewer tokens than standard frame sampling.

Method

A reasoning LLM starts with minimal information, devises a strategy, and executes it using code that probes the video through probing functions.

1Information-Density-Aware Hierarchy

Shot boundaries and a motion score per shot come straight from the compressed bitstream (I-frames, motion vectors, residuals), without decoding frames. Shots are grouped into scenes and clusters for coarse-to-fine navigation.

2Late-Interaction Semantic Retrieval

The LLM searches shots and scenes with text queries. Frames are sampled in proportion to motion, and scores combine token–frame MaxSim with global similarity, which separates visually similar shots.

3Structured Probing with On-Demand Perception

Probing functions (search, sample a shot, zoom, frames in a range, clips) localize evidence coarse to fine. The 2B VLM is called only on the frames the LLM picks, and the reasoning stays in code.

Probing Example

Q: In the oil painting … a few women are stirring the liquid with wooden sticks. What color is the liquid inside the bucket?
